TITLE: MTA 6 Train Line and Hunter College
SOURCE TYPE: Official MTA webpage
URL: https://www.mta.info/maps/subway-line-maps/6-line
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

The 6 is the Lexington Avenue local train in Manhattan and continues into the
Bronx. Hunter College's station is 68 St-Hunter College at East 68th Street and
Lexington Avenue.

The 68 St-Hunter College station has two side platforms, has no in-station
transfer to another subway line, and is listed as ADA accessible.

Nearby useful transfer points on the 6 line include:

- 86 St: transfers to the 4 and 5 except during late nights.
- 59 St: transfers to the N and R, weekday W, 4 and 5 except late nights, and a
  free out-of-system transfer to Lexington Av/63 St for the F and Q.
- 51 St: connection to the E and F during listed service periods.
- Grand Central-42 St: connections to the 4, 5, 7, and S.
- 14 St-Union Sq: connections to the L, N, Q, R, weekday W, 4, and 5.

RELIABILITY NOTE: This page describes the line and stations, but students should
check current MTA schedules and planned service changes before traveling.
