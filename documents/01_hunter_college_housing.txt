TITLE: Hunter College Housing
SOURCE TYPE: Official Hunter College webpage
URL: https://www.hunter.cuny.edu/students/campus-life/residence-life/
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

Hunter College offers affiliated housing in Manhattan. The housing is owned and
operated by Found Study and is intended to provide convenient access to campus,
public transportation, and New York City opportunities.

Hunter has more than 22,000 students but only a limited number of housing
spaces. The application process is highly competitive, housing is not
guaranteed, and students must reapply each year.

The page lists two Hunter housing locations:

- 51st Street Residence: near the 51 St subway station, with standard and
  deluxe double-room options.
- 79th Street Residence: at 334 East 79th Street, a few blocks from Hunter's
  main 68th Street campus.

The page links to eligibility and application instructions, housing policies,
safety information, frequently asked questions, and contact information.

RELIABILITY NOTE: Treat this official page as authoritative for Hunter's current
housing locations and application rules. Availability and room details can
change, so students should verify them before applying.
