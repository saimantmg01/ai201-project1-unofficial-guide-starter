TITLE: Hunter College 68th Street Campus and Directions
SOURCE TYPE: Official Hunter College webpage
URL: https://www.hunter.cuny.edu/about/campus-information/68th-street-campus/
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

Hunter College's main campus is on Manhattan's Upper East Side at 904 Lexington
Avenue, New York, NY 10065.

SUBWAY DIRECTIONS

The 6 train stops directly below the college at 68 St-Hunter College. The
station has an entrance into the college: after exiting the turnstile, riders
can turn right to reach it.

Students can also take the F train to Lexington Avenue/63rd Street and walk
north on Lexington Avenue to East 68th Street.

BUS DIRECTIONS

The M66 travels east on 68th Street and west on 67th Street. The M98, M101,
M102, and M103 travel along Lexington Avenue and Third Avenue. Hunter is at East
68th Street and Lexington Avenue.

The page also provides driving directions from Queens/Long Island, the Bronx,
Westchester, and New Jersey.

RELIABILITY NOTE: This official page is authoritative for the campus location
and basic directions. Riders should use live MTA service information for delays
and temporary route changes.
