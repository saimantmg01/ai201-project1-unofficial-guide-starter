TITLE: MTA Maps for Planning a Hunter College Commute
SOURCE TYPE: Official MTA webpage
URL: https://www.mta.info/maps
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

The MTA maps page provides several tools for comparing commutes to Hunter
College:

- A subway diagram showing typical weekday service.
- A late-night subway diagram.
- Geographic weekday and late-night subway maps.
- A geographic map highlighting accessible stations.
- A large-type subway map.
- Neighborhood maps and text maps for individual subway lines.
- Bus maps for the Bronx, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Queens, and Staten Island.
- Long Island Rail Road and Metro-North diagrams.

Students should use the weekday map for a normal class commute and compare it
with the late-night map if classes, work, or studying may keep them out late.
The accessible-stations map is important for anyone who requires step-free
travel. Borough bus maps can help evaluate housing that is not within walking
distance of a subway station.

RELIABILITY NOTE: Maps describe normal service patterns. Planned work, delays,
and temporary changes require live MTA service information.
