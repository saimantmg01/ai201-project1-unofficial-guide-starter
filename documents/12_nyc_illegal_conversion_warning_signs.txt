TITLE: How to Spot an Illegal Apartment Conversion
SOURCE TYPE: Official NYC Department of Buildings webpage
URL: https://www.nyc.gov/site/buildings/tenant/spot-illegal-conversion.page
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

Illegal dwellings can endanger tenants, neighbors, and first responders. NYC
lists warning signs that renters should investigate before signing or paying:

- A price far below comparable apartments in the area.
- Listings emphasizing a basement or attic that may lack adequate exits.
- Rooms with no windows or unusually small windows.
- A "flex" layout created with walls that may not have required permits.
- Utilities included because the owner may be hiding illegal occupancy.
- Strange layouts, such as a shower installed in a kitchen.
- A landlord who will not disclose the exact address.
- A requirement that tenants receive mail at a separate post office box.
- No written lease, cash-only payments, or an informal month-to-month demand.
- Locked exits or an inadequate way to leave the unit during an emergency.

Students should inspect all exits, verify the address and building history, and
avoid paying before they understand the unit's legal status and lease terms.

RELIABILITY NOTE: This official safety guidance identifies warning signs, but a
warning sign alone does not establish a legal conclusion about a property.
