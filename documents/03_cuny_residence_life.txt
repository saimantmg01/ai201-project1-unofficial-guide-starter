TITLE: CUNY Residence Life
SOURCE TYPE: Official CUNY webpage
URL: https://www.cuny.edu/about/administration/offices/student-affairs/programs-services/housing-residence-life/
RETRIEVED: 2026-06-08

CUNY describes residence life as an opportunity to live in an inclusive
community, meet a diverse group of people, develop communication skills,
participate in campus activities, and build relationships with faculty and
staff.

The page links to campus-sponsored residence halls at several CUNY colleges.
Hunter College is included, with contact information for Hunter housing:

- Phone: 212-396-7136
- Email: living@hunter.cuny.edu

Housing applications are hosted on the individual residence hall or college
websites.

CUNY also lists off-campus resources available to all CUNY students. These
include housing-resource pages from several colleges and third-party providers
such as Educational Housing Services and Found Study.

CUNY states that these links are informational, not a complete list or an
endorsement. Students are strongly encouraged to research every potential
accommodation before making a decision and to review NYC tenant rights and
responsibilities.

RELIABILITY NOTE: Use this page as an official directory. It does not guarantee
availability or recommend a particular third-party provider.
